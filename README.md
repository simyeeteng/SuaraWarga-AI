# SuaraWarga AI

SuaraWarga AI is an AI-powered public service and smart mobility platform designed to help elderly Malaysians access government services and travel independently through natural voice conversations.

Developed under Track T5: AI for Public Services & Smart Cities, the platform combines multilingual speech recognition, dialect-aware Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language Models (LLMs), Optical Character Recognition (OCR), AI decision-making, and smart mobility planning into a single conversation-first solution.

Users can communicate in Hokkien, Cantonese, Malay, or English, while the AI assists with government services such as understanding official letters, completing forms, checking required documents, and planning safer journeys.

By integrating citizen service automation, inclusive AI interaction, and community-driven urban intelligence, SuaraWarga AI aims to improve accessibility, independence, and public service delivery.

## Problem Statement

Malaysia's ageing population faces difficulties when accessing essential public services.

Many elderly citizens struggle with:

- Complex government portals
- Formal government documents
- Digital forms
- Limited digital confidence
- Language and dialect barriers
- Travelling to government offices and healthcare facilities
- Limited access to private transportation

Additionally, many elderly citizens no longer drive or have private transportation, making it challenging to independently reach government offices, healthcare facilities, and community services.

## Solution

SuaraWarga AI addresses these challenges through a voice-first user experience.

Instead of navigating complicated menus or typing information, users can simply speak naturally. Using *Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)*, dialect-aware NLP, and intent recognition, the platform understands user requests and connects them with suitable government service workflows.

The platform combines:

- Multilingual voice interaction
- Dialect-aware AI
- Government service assistance
- Document understanding
- Smart form completion
- Document verification
- AI-powered route planning
- Community walkability mapping

## Key Features
## 1. Voice & Dialect AI Assistant

The Voice & Dialect AI Assistant allows elderly users to communicate with government services using everyday speech.

Supported languages include:

- Hokkien
- Cantonese
- Malay
- English

The assistant uses speech recognition, NLP, and intent recognition to understand user requests and provide relevant assistance.

## 2. Government Letter Interpreter

The Government Letter Interpreter uses OCR and LLM technology to analyse official government documents.

It can help users:

- Understand complex government letters
- Simplify formal information
- Identify important deadlines
- Highlight required actions
- Extract relevant information from documents

This reduces the difficulty elderly users may experience when reading and understanding formal documents.

## 3. Smart Form Assistant

The Smart Form Assistant guides users through government application processes.

Users can provide information through voice conversations, while the system converts their responses into structured information for government forms.

This reduces the need for users to manually type information into complicated digital forms.

## 4. Document Readiness Checker

The Document Readiness Checker helps users verify whether they have prepared the necessary documents before visiting a government agency.

The feature aims to:

- Check required documents
- Identify missing documents
- Reduce unnecessary trips
- Improve preparation before appointments
- Make government service visits more efficient、

## 5. Smart Mobility Assistant

SuaraWarga AI extends beyond digital government services by providing an AI-powered Smart Mobility Assistant for elderly users.

Instead of selecting routes based only on distance or travel time, the AI decision engine evaluates environmental and personal mobility factors.

These factors include:

- Walking distance
- Weather conditions
- Temperature
- Humidity
- UV exposure
- Covered walkways
- Tree coverage
- Personal mobility preferences

## 6. TropicalRoute AI

TropicalRoute AI recommends safer and more comfortable walking routes based on current environmental conditions.

When walking conditions are unsuitable, the system can provide public transport guidance, including:

- Nearby bus stops
- Available routes
- Estimated travel times
- Voice-assisted navigation

This helps elderly users travel more independently while considering Malaysia's tropical climate.

## 7. Community Walkability Mapping

SuaraWarga AI also supports smart city development through Community Walkability Mapping.

Citizens can contribute walkway images, which are analysed using AI-powered computer vision.

The system identifies pedestrian infrastructure and environmental features such as:

- Shaded paths
- Covered walkways
- Bus shelters
- Accessibility infrastructure
- Pedestrian facilities

The collected insights can improve mobility recommendations while providing useful information for local authorities and urban planners.

# System Overview

SuaraWarga AI combines AI-powered public service automation with smart mobility assistance in a single conversation-first platform.

The overall workflow consists of:

- User communicates through voice or provides a document.
- Speech recognition or OCR processes the input.
- NLP and intent recognition determine the user's request.
- AI services process the relevant information.
- The appropriate government service or mobility feature is activated.
- The system provides the result through an accessible, voice-first interaction.
